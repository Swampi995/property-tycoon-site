Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

Property Tycoon does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The game runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, does not contain advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

Data on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves the device unless you back up your phone, in which case the privacy policy of Apple or Google applies to that backup. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Purchases

The game is free to download and offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. We receive no personal or payment information from it. The game only learns whether the unlock is owned, and keeps that on your device.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: swp.mrs@gmail.com.
