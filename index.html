You start with $150,000 and a desk at home. Somewhere on the edge of town there is a tired little house nobody wants. Buy it, fix it up, and decide: rent it out, or sell it and do it again.

Property Tycoon is a business simulation in the spirit of the classic dev-studio tycoons, set in real estate. Grow from one house to a development firm, then to a corporate headquarters with your name on the skyline. Every building is a project: you pick the blueprint, split the effort, choose the crew, and the market grades what you made.

What you do

  • Flip and rent. Buy starter homes on a living city map, renovate them, set the rent, and watch tenants move in. Or list them for sale and move on.
  • Hire a team. Architects, engineers, site managers, brokers, property managers and analysts. They gain experience, need training, and quit when you work them too hard.
  • Research and build. Unlock blueprints from houses to mid-rises. Split effort across structure, interiors, amenities and efficiency, and get an appraisal grade that tells you what buyers wanted.
  • Win auctions. Bid against hidden rivals for bank sales at a fraction of their value, with a deposit on the line.
  • Raise a skyline. High-rises, office towers, skyscrapers and mixed-use towers with shops, offices and homes stacked floor by floor. Bring in investors, take on management contracts, and ride out booms, busts and rate shocks.

No ads, no accounts, and no internet needed. Your game stays on your phone.
