Frequently asked

Where is my save? The game saves automatically on your device every few game days and whenever you leave the app, and keeps a backup copy. It is not synced between devices.

What happens while I am away? When you come back, up to 90 days pass at the speed you left the game on, and a note tells you how your cash changed. A paused game stays paused.

How do I start over? Open the Finance tab and tap New game at the bottom. This replaces your current game.

Why can I not renovate or build? Bigger renovations and every building type are unlocked on the Research tab. On your own you can run one project at a time; hire staff on the Office tab to run more at once.

Why was tax taken from my sale? Selling a property within a year of buying it pays 20% tax on the profit, and every purchase has 2% closing costs. Holding longer avoids the tax.

How do auctions work? Bidding needs a 10% deposit, which comes back if someone outbids you. If you win, the rest of the price plus closing costs is paid when the auction closes, so keep the cash ready.

Why did the game end? If your cash stays below zero for 30 days, the company goes bankrupt. You get a warning after two weeks: sell something or take a loan against a property.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock? The home-office years are free: buying, renovating, flipping, renting, your first hires and early research. One purchase unlocks the storefront agency, the development firm and the corporate HQ, and every building and upgrade they open. It is a one-time purchase, and there is nothing else to buy.

I bought the full game, but it is locked. Open Settings from the title screen or the pause menu and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Is there any real money involved? Only the optional purchase that unlocks the full game. Everything inside the game is simulated, and it is not financial or investment advice.
